Changelog

3.4.0 · 2026-11-10

  • Secondary button (Button): .sys-btn--secondary, a newsprint block with black type that turns black on hover. It pairs with the black button on white; the ghost stays the choice on newsprint or a color. It comes from jonesco.com’s hero, which already used it.

3.3.1 · 2026-11-08

  • Back to top: the title it focuses no longer shows a focus ring. Safari outlined the heading after the jump; anything with tabindex="-1" now takes focus without one.

3.3.0 · 2026-11-08

  • Back to top (new page): .sys-to-top, a floating black pill with a white stroke, an up arrow and “Top”. It appears once the opening block scrolls away and returns to the top of the page. First used on the style specimens page.

3.2.0 · 2026-11-08

  • Accent tag (Tag): .sys-tag--accent, a magenta pill with white type for what’s new or featured. One per group, never a status. It started as the New tag on the style specimens page.

3.1.0 · 2026-11-08

  • Card hairline (Card): a 1px white line (--paper) now separates the art from the newsprint body. It started on the style specimens page and is on jonesco.com’s work cards too. Sites pinned to 3.0.0 or earlier are unaffected.

3.0.0 · 2026-11-08

  • Tags are pills with no border (Tag). Three kinds: dark (.sys-tag, black with yellow type), light (.sys-tag--light, newsprint with black type) and white (.sys-tag--white, for newsprint or a color behind it). Two sizes: small, 24px, the default; medium (.sys-tag--md), 32px. Warning stays a thin outline, now a pill. .sys-tag--plain still works as the light tag.
  • Major: tags change shape and size everywhere. Tags and the toggle are the only exceptions to square corners. Sites pinned to 2.x are unaffected.
  • Card (new page): .sys-card is art on top and the words below on newsprint, with no border. Optional id, sentence-case title, text, and a foot with a tag and a “View” label pinned to the bottom. .sys-card--sm for dense grids. It comes from jonesco.com’s work cards and is now used on a second page.
  • Switch is now Content switcher. .sys-content-switcher and .sys-content-switcher--tabs (renamed page). .sys-switch keeps working as a deprecated alias, so nothing changes on sites that use it.
  • Icon only button: .sys-btn--icon makes any button size a square with one glyph (24, 20 or 16px), always with a tooltip and an aria-label.
  • Button sizes: today’s button is large (64px) and stays the default. New medium (.sys-btn--md, 40px) and small (.sys-btn--sm, 32px) match the medium and small content switchers.
  • Content switcher sizes: small stays the default; medium (.sys-content-switcher--md) is 40px tall to match a text field beside it.
  • Toggle (new page): .sys-toggle, one setting on or off. A round knob in a pill track with a thin black border that fills black when on; the label names the setting, never its state. The knob slides with a slight overshoot and stretches while pressed. Works on black.
  • Text input (new page): .sys-input in a .sys-field with a caps label, help and error text. Text fields are newsprint with no border, 40px tall to line up with a medium content switcher; focus draws a thin black border, errors draw a magenta rule around the field with the message in magenta under it. Works for textareas, and a search box (type="search") leads with a magnifying glass.
  • Tooltip (new page): .sys-tooltip with data-tip, a black label with white type (white with black type on black) on hover and keyboard focus, with a tail whose tip sits 4px from the control. Below, above, left or right; above or below can line up at the start, middle or end. Icon button tooltips change from yellow to the same black.
  • Docs: content switcher and toggle examples respond to clicks.
  • Resources in the docs menu: Style specimens, a reference page of illustration and motion drawn in code. It uses the system and its cards; it isn’t part of the system.
  • Docs on phones: the sidebar is a menu, previous and next links close every page, and nothing scrolls sideways.

2.1.0 · 2026-11-06

  • Table (new page): .sys-table is a newsprint panel with padding around a data table. Caps headers on the thick rule, rows on thin rules, an optional bold total under a thick rule, every column left-aligned with tabular digits. Inside it the Working tag is white so it stays visible.
  • Financial Dashboard example rebuilt: its savings accounts and top stocks and funds are tables.
  • Docs: the docs’ own reference tables are styled only as direct children of the page, so they no longer reach into examples.

2.0.0 · 2026-10-24

  • Body type is Trade Gothic. --font-body is Trade Gothic regular, with its oblique and bold, in place of Libre Franklin. Jonesco now uses only Trade Gothic. Four trimmed WOFF2 cuts join fonts/, with the same cap-centering fix as the display cuts. See Typography.
  • Display and label fallbacks are Trade Gothic, then the system sans. Sites no longer need the Google Fonts link for Libre Franklin; remove it when upgrading.
  • Major: body copy looks different everywhere. Sites pinned to earlier versions are unaffected.
