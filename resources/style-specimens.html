Where code falls short

These need real files, a different tool or a person.

  • Photoreal images and painterly brushworkCode can fake texture and grain. It can't paint. Use photography, image generation or an illustrator.
  • Finished character illustrationSimple rigs and mascots work. Detailed, expressive characters with real anatomy don't.
  • Long character animationShort loops are fine. Acting, lip sync and long scenes aren't. A Lottie or video file you supply can be played and controlled.
  • Modeled 3DPrimitives, distance fields and three.js scenes work. Detailed models need files.
  • Your brand assetsLogos, licensed fonts and product photos need the originals.
  • Older browsersScroll timelines, view transitions and @property degrade to a still frame where they aren't supported.
