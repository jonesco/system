Principles

How a Jonesco site should feel. The values live in the foundations; these are the reasons behind them.

Look

  • Black, white, newsprint. Newsprint is “paper”: use it behind artwork and printed things, and to block in or contain a section. Never as the whole page background.
  • One accent per site. Magenta is the default. It marks hover and the one active thing; it’s not decoration.
  • Yellow is the highlight. Black tags with yellow type, hover on black, text selection.
  • Thick rules for structure, thin rules for lists.
  • Square corners on anything with a border. Photos of rounded real objects can stay rounded.
  • No shadows or gradients on UI. The only shadow is a soft one under a photographed product.

Type

  • Headlines: Trade Gothic Bold Condensed No. 20, caps, tight, big. Condensed type reads small, so size it up.
  • Labels: Trade Gothic Bold No. 2, caps, slightly open: nav, buttons, tags, list labels.
  • Body: Libre Franklin, sentence case.
  • Curly quotes and apostrophes in copy. American spelling.

Layout

  • One centered 1200px column. Everything lines up on its edges, full-width bands included.
  • Separate sections with a thick rule or a switch between white and black, not both.
  • Pages close on black: a black band runs straight into the black footer.
  • Headings get the same space above and below everywhere.

Behavior

  • One highlight at a time. Hover color changes in .15s. Respect reduced motion.

Voice and art

  • Short, specific, a little dry. Hand-drawn work is the hero; line art sits on newsprint; logos reverse to pure white on black.
