Logo

JONES© in three widths. Same letters, same ©; pick the widest one that fits.

Versions

VersionUseRatio
Full · JONES©The default, wherever it fits: site headers, documents, invoices, merch.3.47 : 1
Short · JNS©When the full logo would drop below its minimum size, such as narrow headers.2.41 : 1
Mark · J©Square and tiny spaces: favicons, app and home-screen icons, avatars, stamps.1.35 : 1

Black and white

Black on light backgrounds, white on black. Those are the only two colors for the logo itself.

The mark can sit on a solid accent block as an icon: the docs’ own favicon is the white J© on magenta (icons/).

Space and size

  • Clear space: at least a quarter of the logo’s height on every side.
  • Minimum size: Full 24px tall on screen, Short 20px, Mark 16px. Below that, step down a version.
  • All three files share one height, so swapping versions keeps the baseline and the © in place.
Do

Use the SVG files as they are, scaled evenly, in black or white.

Don’t

Stretch, outline, recolor, add effects, or retype the letters. Don’t put the logo in a rounded box.
