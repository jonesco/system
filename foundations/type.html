Case

One rule: caps for the hero, sentence case for titles you read, caps for short labels.

Sentence case

  • Capitalize only the first word and proper names: Netspend, Wayblazer, MagicStaff, CPay, Yōkai.
  • Acronyms stay capitalized: POC, AI, UX.
Do

One caps hero per page; tile and section titles in sentence case under it.

Don’t

Set a whole grid of tile titles, or every heading in an article, in condensed caps. It shouts.

Stipulations

  • Condensed caps read smaller than their size: set headlines larger than you would a regular face.
  • The fonts carry a metric fix so caps sit centered in their boxes; don’t nudge them with padding.
  • Characters outside the trimmed font (like Ō) fall back to Libre Franklin. The Art Dept fonts include a custom Ō.
