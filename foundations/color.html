Color

Black, white and newsprint do the work. One accent and one highlight carry meaning.

Ink--ink #000
Text, rules, header, footer, bands.
Paper--paper #fff
Page background.
Newsprint--newsprint #edebe6
Behind artwork; ghost buttons. A warm light gray, a step darker than the gray track (17.6:1 with black type).
Accent--accent #e4148c
Hover on white; the one active thing.
Highlight--highlight #ffe500
Tags, hover on black, selection.
Muted--muted #555
Secondary text.

Charts have their own color: see Data & charts.

Usage

Do

Use the accent for the one thing that’s active or hovered: a tile title, a chosen size, a pressed button.

Don’t

Use the accent as decoration, or put two accent-colored things side by side.

Do

Put scanned line art on newsprint with mix-blend-mode: multiply so the paper reads as paper.

Don’t

Use newsprint as a page or section background.
