Stipulations

  • Figures use the condensed display face with tabular digits, sized up because condensed reads small.
  • One mark per view. .sys-mark is for the single headline number (one highlight at a time). It works on white and on black.
  • Gains and losses carry a sign (+ or −) and words; the figure stays ink.
