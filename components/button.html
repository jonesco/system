Stipulations

  • One primary (black) button per area; the ghost is the secondary.
  • Square corners, always.
  • Buying goes through a black button that names where it goes: “Buy on Amazon”, “Buy now”.
  • Icon only needs words somewhere else: a tooltip and an aria-label saying the same thing. Use it for actions people know by their icon (download, copy, add, close); anything else gets a label. Glyphs are 2px strokes with square ends, drawn in currentColor.
  • Icon only button or icon button? A .sys-btn--icon is a full button, black or outlined, that sits in a row of buttons. A icon button (.sys-icon-btn) is a quiet glyph with no box, for inside content and headers.
  • Size to the row. Large stands alone as a page’s call to action. In a toolbar or a row of controls, match the button to its neighbors: medium beside a medium switcher or a text input, small beside a small switcher.
