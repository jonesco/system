Stipulations

  • One bar per example, along its bottom edge. It belongs to the example it drives, not to the page; page-wide settings go in the header or a toggle.
  • Label the slider with what it changes (“Angle”, “Assembly”), and show the value at the right end. When the ends mean more than the number, name the current end instead (“Assembled”, “Exploded”).
  • The knob is the one round, magenta thing. The track stays gray; don’t fill it up to the knob.
  • Buttons say what they do in a word or two: Step, Replay, Slow. A mode that stays on (Slow) uses aria-pressed and fills white while on.
  • Frame strip: one dot per frame of a short loop. Mark the frames that teach something (a smear, a key pose) with .is-marked; it’s decorative, so hide it from screen readers and say the frame in the readout.
  • Labels and buttons are Trade Gothic caps; readouts may use --font-mono when the digits change while you watch.
  • Anything that moves on its own stops for reduced motion; the controls still work and step instead.
