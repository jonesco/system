Stipulations

  • The label names the setting, never its state: “Motion”, not “Motion on”. The track shows the state.
  • It acts at once. No Save button after a toggle; if a change needs confirming, use a checkbox in a form instead.
  • Toggle or content switcher? One setting, on or off, is a toggle. Choosing between named views is a content switcher.
  • Track first, label after. The track is a pill, like a tag, an exception to square corners; no accent color for on. Black is the one active state, as in the content switcher.
  • The label turns magenta on hover (yellow on black). Disabled toggles drop to 40% and don’t respond.
  • Motion: the knob slides in 260ms with a slight overshoot, and stretches toward where it’s going while pressed. With reduced motion it just switches.
  • Use a real <button> with role="switch" and flip aria-checked in script; the styles follow the attribute.
