A 40px field on newsprint with no border. Focus draws a thin black border.

Search, beside a content switcher

A search field leads with a magnifying glass; type="search" is all it needs. Without a visible label, give the field an aria-label. At 40px it matches a medium content switcher.

Stipulations

  • Text fields are newsprint with no border. Every text field, search box and textarea fills with newsprint; the border appears only on focus. On a newsprint panel (table, card, paper) the field turns white so it still shows.
  • 40px tall. The same height as a medium content switcher and close to a 44px touch target with its label; beside a switcher, use the medium size so the row lines up.
  • Square corners. Focus draws a thin black border (the space is held by a clear border, so nothing shifts); no glow and no second ring.
  • Labels sit above in caps. Placeholder text is a hint, never the label.
  • Errors are magenta: a thin magenta rule around the field and a bold magenta message under it that says what to do (“Add the rest of the address”). Never red. Set aria-invalid="true" and point aria-describedby at the message.
  • Body text is 16px so phones don’t zoom in when the field gets focus.
