Stipulations

  • No border and no rule under the title. The newsprint is the edge. The only line is the 1px white hairline between the art and the words; keep it even when the art is pale. On a newsprint page section, use a Tile instead.
  • Card or Tile? A card carries a description and a foot; a tile is a picture and a title over the thick rule.
  • Title is sentence case in Bold No. 2 (see Typography · Case).
  • A linked card turns its “View” label magenta on hover and scales the image to 1.04; the title stays black. Live artwork doesn’t scale.
  • Status or category goes in a tag in the foot; the Working tag turns white so it shows on the newsprint.
  • Cards in a row are the same height; the foot sits at the bottom of each.
  • Three across on desktop, two on tablet, one on phones. Small cards fill as many 272px columns as fit.
