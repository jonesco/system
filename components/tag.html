Tag

A small black block with a yellow label. Marks status or an identifier.

Status levels

When a page needs more than one level of status, step down in weight instead of adding colors.

Critical Warning Working

Stipulations

  • Critical is always the plain tag: black block, yellow type. Magenta type on black or a magenta block were tried and dropped.
  • The count of missing items next to a chart (“2 not recorded”) uses the plain tag too, because it’s part of the critical alert.
  • A notification holding a critical tag gets a thick black rule around it, not a colored one.
