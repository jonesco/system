Tag

A pill with no border that marks a status, a category or an id. Dark is black with yellow type; light is newsprint with black type; white is for newsprint and color backgrounds.

New · Vol. 1 Case Study #5487

White

On newsprint or any color, use the white tag (.sys-tag--white) so it stands off the background. A light tag inside a newsprint panel (table, card) or a notification turns white on its own.

Sizes

Small (24px) is the default. Medium (.sys-tag--md, 32px) is the height of a small content switcher, for a tag in a row with one.

Do

Use for one short status, category or id: New, Vol. 1, Case Study #5487, SVG.

Don’t

Use as a button or for sentences. A tag never takes a click.

Status levels

When a page needs more than one level of status, step down in weight instead of adding colors: Critical is the dark tag, Warning a pill outline, Working the light tag.

Stipulations

  • Tags are pills. Only tags and the toggle are rounded; everything else stays square, so a tag can never be mistaken for a button.
  • No border on dark, light or white tags. The Warning outline is the one exception.
  • Light on white, white on anything else. On a white page use the light tag; on newsprint or a color use the white tag, so the tag always shows against what’s behind it. Dark works on any light background.
  • Critical is always the dark tag: black, yellow type. Magenta type on black or a magenta block were tried and dropped.
  • The count of missing items next to a chart (“2 not recorded”) uses the dark tag too, because it’s part of the critical alert.
  • A critical tag in a notification sits inside the inline notification container (yellow rule on pale yellow).
  • Small everywhere by default; medium only beside a small content switcher or another 32px control.
  • .sys-tag--plain is the 2.x name for the light tag and still works.
